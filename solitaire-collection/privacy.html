Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the app ("we", "the App") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our solitaire card games (Klondike, Spider, Yukon).

Information We Collect

The App may collect the following categories of information through third-party services (ad mediation and analytics providers) used within the App:

  • App activity (e.g. interactions with the app and with ads, crash logs, diagnostics)
  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • App performance data
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level)

We do not require account creation and do not collect your name, email address, or payment information directly.

How We Use Information

  • To display and measure advertising, including personalized advertising
  • To understand app performance and fix issues
  • To improve gameplay features over time

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party advertising and analytics SDKs to serve ads and measure app usage. Providers may include, but are not limited to, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Firebase Crashlytics. These providers may combine data collected from this App with data collected from other apps and websites in order to serve targeted advertising and measure its effectiveness. They may collect and process data as described in their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy

Tracking and Your Consent (iOS)

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, ads will still be shown but will not be personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Data Security

Data transmitted through third-party SDKs used in the App is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information.

Your Choices

You can request deletion of data associated with your device identifier by contacting us at the email below. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
