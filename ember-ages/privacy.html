Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ember Ages: Idle War ("the App", "we", "us"), published by Molfar Digital, collects, uses and shares information when you play the game on Android.

Summary

  • The App has no accounts and no real-money purchases. It shows ads provided by Google AdMob.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to measure how the game is played and to receive crash reports. These services are part of how the App works and cannot be turned off inside the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or payment information.

Information We Collect

Through Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob the App may collect:

  • Gameplay events: when a battle starts and ends and how it ended (won, lost or abandoned), its duration, the wave and gate health reached, the number of units spawned, coins earned and average frame rate; which upgrades, warrior roles and techniques you buy or open and what they cost; which rewards you claim; which screens you open; your language choice; and when you tap an offer to double a reward.
  • Ad events: when an ad is loaded, shown, clicked or closed, and when a reward is earned from a rewarded ad.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: technical information about the state of the App when it crashes or fails, together with simple context such as the app version, the battle number and the screen you were on.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region level).
  • Identifiers: a randomly generated app instance identifier and, where available on your device, the advertising ID, which AdMob also uses to show and measure ads.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked content, settings and counters such as the number of finished battles) is stored in the App's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

How We Use Information

  • To understand how players move through the game and to balance and improve it
  • To find and fix crashes and other technical problems
  • To measure overall usage of the App
  • To show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To decide when to ask you to rate the App on Google Play

Ads are provided by Google AdMob and may be personalized depending on your region and consent settings.

Legal Basis (EEA, UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process analytics and crash data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the App. Ad personalization and the related use of device identifiers rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent form described below.

Ads and Consent

The App shows two kinds of ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch to double a reward, and full-screen ads between battles. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. The App does not yet have a button to reopen the form; you can see it again by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by reinstalling the App.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob, all provided by Google. Google processes the data described above on our behalf and under its own terms. The App also uses the Google Play In-App Review API, which lets Google Play show its own rating dialog; we do not see what you enter there.

Data Security

Data sent by the App to Firebase and AdMob is encrypted in transit.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings.
  • You can reset your ad consent choice by clearing the App's data or reinstalling it, and the consent form will be shown again where required.
  • You can stop all data collection by the App by uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us at the email below and include your app instance identifier or advertising ID if you want us to find your data. We can only locate data that you identify this way.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. If we add real-money purchases, accounts or new data-collecting services, this policy will be updated before they ship.
