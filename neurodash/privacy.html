Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 5, 2026

Hi! I'm Molfar Digital, the developer of NeuroDash. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, or shared when you use NeuroDash on Android or iOS.

Quick Version

  • No in-app account is required to play NeuroDash.
  • The app uses Google AdMob to show ads.
  • The app uses Firebase services for analytics and app-related measurement.
  • Some data may be processed by third-party SDKs for ads, analytics, diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention.
  • Data is transmitted over a secure connection where supported by the relevant services.

Data That May Be Collected

Depending on your device, platform, region, consent choices, and app version, NeuroDash and its third-party partners may collect or process the following types of data:

Identifiers

Device ID, advertising identifiers, and other technical identifiers. This may include device-level identifiers used for advertising, analytics, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and app functionality. On iOS, access to certain advertising identifiers may depend on App Tracking Transparency permission. On Android, advertising identifier availability depends on device and privacy settings.

Usage Data

Product interaction and advertising data. This may include app launches, taps, gameplay interactions, ad views, ad performance signals, and other information about how users interact with the app and ads.

Diagnostics

Crash data and performance data. This may include crash logs, performance signals, device/app state, and technical diagnostics used to keep the app and integrated SDKs stable and reliable.

Approximate Location

NeuroDash does not require GPS permission and does not need precise location to work. However, advertising and measurement providers may process approximate location, such as location inferred from IP address or regional/device signals, for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

How Data May Be Used

  • Third-party advertising: to show ads, measure ad performance, limit fraud and abuse, and support ad-related services.
  • Analytics: to understand app usage, feature engagement, crashes, performance, and general audience behavior.
  • App functionality: to keep the app and SDKs working correctly.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance: to detect invalid traffic, abuse, fraud, technical issues, or policy violations.

Ads and Consent

NeuroDash uses Google AdMob to display ads. Depending on your region, you may be shown consent options related to personalized ads, measurement, or data processing.

On iOS, the app may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing certain advertising identifiers or using data for tracking purposes.

Third-Party Services

NeuroDash uses third-party services that may process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase — Firebase Privacy and Security
  • How Google uses information from apps using its services
  • Apple Game Center — Apple Privacy Policy

Game Center and Leaderboards

On iOS, NeuroDash may use Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Game Center is provided by Apple and is subject to Apple's own privacy practices. NeuroDash does not require users to create a separate in-app account.

Your Choices

  • You can change ad consent choices where available in your region.
  • You can manage tracking permission on iOS in system settings.
  • You can manage advertising identifier settings on Android where supported by your device.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop further data collection by the app from your device.

Children

NeuroDash is not specifically directed to children. The app may show ads and use third-party SDKs as described in this policy.
