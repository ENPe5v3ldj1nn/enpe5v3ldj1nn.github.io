Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sokoban: Box Push Puzzle ("the App", "we") collects, uses, and shares information when you play the game.

Summary

  • The App has no accounts, no ads, and no in-app purchases.
  • Your game progress (unlocked levels and stars) is stored only on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase to measure how the game is played and to receive crash reports.

Information We Collect

Through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics the App may collect:

  • Gameplay events: which level was started or completed, the number of moves and pushes, the stars earned, how often undo and restart are used, and the length of a play session.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: technical information about the app state when it crashes or fails.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address (country or region level).
  • Identifiers: a randomly generated app instance identifier and, where available on your device, the advertising ID.

We do not ask for and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.

How We Use Information

  • To understand which levels are too easy or too hard and improve the game
  • To find and fix crashes and other technical problems
  • To measure overall usage of the App

We do not use this information for advertising and we do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. Google processes the data described above on our behalf and according to its own terms.

  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Google Privacy Policy

Data Security

Data sent by the App to Firebase is encrypted in transit.

Your Choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. You can request deletion of data associated with your device by contacting us at the email below. Uninstalling the App removes all progress stored on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
